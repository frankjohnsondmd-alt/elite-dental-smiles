Custom Night Guards for Teeth Grinding

Grinding and clenching can quietly wear down teeth. A custom night guard helps protect your smile before damage turns into bigger dental work.

Published August 13, 2026  |  Elite Dental

Many people grind or clench their teeth without realizing it. The habit often happens during sleep, which means the first signs may show up at a dental visit: flattened tooth edges, tiny cracks, chipped enamel, gumline notches, sore jaw muscles, or teeth that feel sensitive for no obvious reason.

A custom night guard is a small appliance worn over the teeth while you sleep. It does not magically erase stress or stop every clenching habit, but it can create a protective barrier so your teeth are not taking the full force night after night. For patients in Dandridge, Jefferson City, and nearby East Tennessee communities, it is often one of the simplest ways to prevent avoidable wear.

Signs You May Be Grinding or Clenching

Tooth grinding, also called bruxism, can be obvious or subtle. Some patients wake up with jaw tightness, headaches near the temples, or sore chewing muscles. Others notice chipped front teeth, rough edges, or fillings and crowns that seem to break earlier than expected.

You may also feel tooth sensitivity to cold, pressure, or brushing near the gumline. Grinding can make small cracks worse, strain the jaw joints, and overload dental work. If a spouse or partner hears grinding sounds at night, that is another strong clue.

Worn EdgesFlat, shiny, or shortened teeth can point to repeated grinding.
Jaw SorenessMorning tightness or temple headaches may come from clenching.
Cracks and ChipsHeavy forces can fracture enamel, fillings, crowns, or bonding.
Sensitive TeethPressure and gumline wear can make teeth more reactive.

Why Custom Fit Matters

Store bought boil-and-bite guards can be tempting because they are quick and inexpensive. They may help some people in the short term, but they are not designed around your exact bite. A bulky or loose guard can feel uncomfortable, interfere with sleep, or put uneven pressure on the jaw.

A custom night guard is made from a scan or impression of your teeth. The goal is a secure fit, proper thickness, and a bite surface that protects teeth without adding unnecessary strain. Patients are also more likely to wear a guard that feels stable and does not pop out during the night.

A Night Guard Protects Teeth. It Does Not Replace Diagnosis.

Grinding can be linked to stress, sleep disruption, bite changes, airway concerns, medication effects, or jaw joint issues. A dental exam helps confirm whether a guard is appropriate and whether anything else needs attention.

When a Night Guard Can Save You Money

Grinding damage is usually cumulative. One night of clenching may not break a tooth, but years of heavy force can weaken enamel, crack molars, wear through bonding, and shorten the life of crowns or veneers. Once tooth structure is gone, it does not grow back.

That is why a custom guard can be a smart preventive investment. Protecting teeth now may reduce the risk of crowns, root canals, emergency visits, and repeated repairs later. It is especially important if you already have large fillings, crowns, veneers, implants, bonding, or a history of cracked teeth.

How to Care for a Night Guard

Rinse the guard after wearing it, brush it gently with a soft toothbrush, and let it dry before storing it in its case. Avoid hot water, which can warp the material. Bring it to dental visits so the fit and wear pattern can be checked.

If the guard starts feeling loose, tight, cracked, or uneven, do not keep forcing it. Dental work, tooth movement, or normal appliance wear can change the fit. A quick check can prevent irritation and help you know when replacement makes sense.

Common Questions About Night Guards

What does a night guard do?

It creates a protective layer between upper and lower teeth to reduce damage from nighttime grinding or clenching.

Can a night guard stop grinding?

Usually it protects teeth from the effects of grinding rather than stopping the habit itself. The cause may still need to be discussed.

Is a custom guard better than a store bought guard?

For many patients, yes. A custom guard is usually more stable, less bulky, and designed for your bite.

How long does a night guard last?

It depends on how heavily you grind, how well you clean it, and whether your bite changes. Some last years, while heavy grinders may wear through them sooner.
